Definition

Multimedia tags add audio and video to web pages. Browsers can display controls to play and pause media files.

Attributes

AttributeRole
controlsDisplays playback controls.
autoplayRequests automatic playback.
loopRepeats the media.
mutedMutes the media.

Example (code)

<video controls width="320">
    <source src="video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

<audio controls>
    <source src="audio.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>

Example (result)



Add your own video.mp4 and audio.mp3 files to play media.
